SplitDish Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

SplitDish respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the SplitDish app.

Information We Collect

SplitDish does not require an account and does not directly collect or store personal information such as your name, email address, contacts, or location.

Receipt information entered or scanned within SplitDish is processed within the app and is not uploaded to or stored on SplitDish servers.

Advertising

SplitDish uses Google AdMob to provide advertising. Google may collect and process information such as device information, advertising identifiers, and information related to ad interactions in accordance with Google's privacy policies.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchases

SplitDish offers optional in-app purchases. Purchases are processed through Apple's App Store and are subject to Apple's privacy policies and terms.

Third-Party Services

SplitDish uses third-party services necessary to provide advertising and in-app purchase functionality. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

SplitDish is not directed toward children under the age of 13. SplitDish does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
